## F. Responsible AI risks

| Risk | Who may be affected? | Potential impact | Possible engineering control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unsupported or misleading output | Users relying on the system | User may act on information that is wrong or not sufficiently supported | Source verification, clear uncertainty, testing |
| Privacy / sensitive information | Users whose data is entered or inferred | Personal information may be exposed or used unexpectedly | Avoid sensitive input, data minimisation, access controls |
| Bias or unequal performance | Different user groups or content creators | Output quality or visibility may differ unfairly | Representative testing where feasible, monitor differences, disclose limitations |
